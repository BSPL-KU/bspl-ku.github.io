Aug 6, 2026

Summary: To avoid stimulus-specific confounding variables when changing video stimuli in longitudinal pediatric fMRI, this study decided to maintain the exact same two video stimuli (Inscapes and Partly Cloudy) across all scanning visits.

Vanderwal, Tamara, et al. “Inscapes: A movie paradigm to improve compliance in functional magnetic resonance imaging.” Neuroimage 122 (2015): 222-232.
