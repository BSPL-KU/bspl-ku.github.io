---
title: "[Brainstorming] Can we align brain representations with a semantically structured video-text embedding space?"
date: 2026-10-27
---

Summary: This presentation proposes a framework that combines subject-adaptive fMRI encoding with semantically grounded multimodal alignment (using video-text spaces like LanguageBind and InternVideo2) to improve the discriminability of dynamic visual events and enable open-ended language decoding from continuous, movie-watching brain activity.
