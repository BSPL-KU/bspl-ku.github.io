Our consortium was awarded a new four-year grant, beginning in April 2026, from the Institute of Information & Communications Technology Planning & Evaluation (IITP), funded by the Korean government’s Ministry of Science and ICT (MSIT).

Subproject (2): Development of AI learning technologies based on neuroscience, cognitive science, and biological learning principles
세부 (2) 뇌 인지과학과 생물학적 학습 원리에 기반한 인공지능 학습 기술 개발
