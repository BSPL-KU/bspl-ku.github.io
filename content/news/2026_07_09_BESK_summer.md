Minwoo was invited as an oral presenter and received an award!  
“Utility of Reliability-Aware Rejection in Camera- and IMU-Based Respiratory Rate Estimation”, Minwoo Lee, Chae Lynn Kim, Jungwoo Choi, Jong-Hwan Lee  

Sunghwan was invited for an oral presentation and received an award!  
"Diagnosing Neural-Signal Dependence in Subject-General fMRI-to-Language Decoding", Sung Hwan Lee, Jihun Kim, Chae Lynn Kim, Ji Yun Park, Jae-eon Kang, Jong-Hwan Lee
